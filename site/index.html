Download Tracks for Android

Version 0.2.0, about 35 MB, Android 7 or newer. Older 32-bit phone? Get the build that runs on every phone (94 MB).

Install it

  1. Open the downloaded fileFrom the browser's downloads, tap tracks.apk.
  2. Allow this one installAndroid asks once to allow installs from your browser. Tracks isn't on the Play Store yet, so this is how it gets on your phone.
  3. Tap Install, then OpenUpdates install the same way and keep your data.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. The app never goes online: your transactions live only on your phone. Back up to Google Drive from inside the app when you want to, or let Android's own backup carry Tracks to a new phone.

Check the download

Every Tracks build is signed with the same key. Its SHA-256 certificate digest is ca7143b8e365a457ffda4183684814c4601a39908ac028497c0b83540fee7cba. Android refuses an update signed by anyone else.
