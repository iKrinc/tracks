# Tracks

A personal money tracker for India. Type "chai 20 gpay", share a bank SMS or
a GPay screenshot, and Tracks fills in the rest. No account, no ads, no
analytics, no network calls: everything stays on your phone.

**Download:** [tracks.apk](https://github.com/iKrinc/tracks/releases/latest/download/tracks.apk)
(Android 7+, most phones) or
[tracks-universal.apk](https://github.com/iKrinc/tracks/releases/latest/download/tracks-universal.apk)
(every phone, larger). Not on the Play Store; Android asks once to allow the
install.

## What it does

- Quick add, SMS share, screenshot OCR (on-device), batch review of SMS threads
- Accounts, cards, loans with EMI interest split, IOUs, bills and reminders
- Budgets, reports, payday forecast, funds (a wedding or a trip, tracked
  apart, synced into your timeline only if you want)
- Rules learned from your corrections, app lock, Google Drive backup
